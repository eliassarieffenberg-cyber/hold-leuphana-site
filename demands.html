Demands

These demands are grounded in Leuphana's own institutional documents. The university's Sustainability Guidelines require that all external cooperation be evaluated against its mission statement.

Demands to the University:

  1. Immediately suspending the Non-EU Exchange Programme with the Academic College of Tel Aviv-Yaffo, pending a full review of that institution's compliance with Leuphana's Sustainability Guidelines and mission statement.
  2. Not renewing the Visiting Fellow Programme with researchers institutionally affiliated with the Hebrew University of Jerusalem or other complicit Israeli academic institutions.
  3. Suspending the joint research project between the Institute of Sustainability Psychology at Leuphana and Hebrew University of Jerusalem faculty, and conducting a review of its funding sources and institutional entanglements.
  4. Removing the active promotion of the Israel-Lower Saxony Exchange (ILSSE) and the Minerva Foundation from Leuphana's academic advising channels, funding databases, and doctoral support materials, until both programmes have been assessed for compliance with the university's own ethical commitments.
  5. Establishing a review commission tasked with conducting a full audit of all existing institutional ties to Israeli academic institutions — including those not documented in this report — and publishing its findings. This commission should include representatives of the students and workers who have raised these concerns.

Demands to the Student Parliament (StuPa):

Regarding academic freedom and the definition of antisemitism, we demand the following:

  1. Formally ceasing the application of the IHRA working definition of antisemitism in any institutional decision-making, including decisions regarding the use of university facilities, event approvals, and funding allocations.
  2. Adopting the Jerusalem Declaration on Antisemitism (JDA) as the university's working framework for identifying and addressing antisemitism on campus. The JDA offers a rigorous, scholarly definition that protects Jewish students and staff from genuine antisemitism while preserving the right to criticise the policies of the Israeli state and to engage in solidarity with the Palestinian people.
  3. Issuing a public statement affirming that participation in campaigns for Palestinian rights, including calls for the suspension of institutional ties with complicit Israeli universities, does not constitute antisemitism under any definition applied by Leuphana.
  4. Establishing connections with Palestinian Universities in the occupied West Bank, East Jerusalem, and Gaza.

The mission statement commits Leuphana to humanism, responsibility for future generations, and shaping society towards justice. We are demanding the university comply with its own rules and regulations.
